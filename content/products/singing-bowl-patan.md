Hand-hammered in Patan, Nepal.

This bowl was made by Raju Shakya in his workshop in Patan's Durbar Square. It was hammered cold — not cast — from a copper and tin alloy. The resonance was tested continuously throughout the making process. This bowl has its own note.

Includes a traditional wooden striker.
