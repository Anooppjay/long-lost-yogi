Full-grain leather, hand-tooled in Fes.

This bag was made by Hassan Al-Fassi in his workshop in the Fes el-Bali medina. It took four days. The leather is sourced from the Chouara tannery cooperative. The dye is natural — saffron and pomegranate. The stitching is hand-waxed linen.

There is no machine involved in this bag at any point from hide to finished article.
