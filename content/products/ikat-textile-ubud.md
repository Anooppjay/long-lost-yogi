Hand-woven on a backstrap loom in Ubud, Bali.

This textile was made by Wayan Sari over three weeks. The warp threads were resist-dyed before weaving began — the pattern was carried in thread before it existed in fabric. The design belongs to her family and has been used in temple ceremonies in the Klungkung region for three generations.

Documented ceremonial provenance included with every textile.
